Lots of Mayhem – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Lots of Mayhem (package name com.peterrombakis.lotsofmayhem) handles your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Lots of Mayhem does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not create accounts, and it has no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the game saves a few small files in its own private storage on your device. These contain only game data:

  • Your career progress (current day, in-game money, upgrades, and cosmetic items you own or wear).
  • Your high scores and best results.
  • Your settings (graphics quality, FPS display, screen shake, and chosen map).

These files never leave your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Internet, ads, and analytics

The game does not request internet access and works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash reporting services, and no in-app purchases. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

Device permissions

The game does not request any special Android permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access).

Google Play

If you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of operating the Play Store, under Google's own Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information (for example, by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
